# NovaPay: Smart Personal Finance & Expense Tracking App
**UI / UX DESIGN – Task 1**  
**Designer:** Angel S S

---

## 📌 Problem Statement
**NovaPay** is a secure mobile payment app that makes digital transactions simple and fast. Users can send money, split group expenses, scan QR codes, pay bills, track spending, and manage their account through a clean, user-friendly interface.

---

## 🗺️ User Flow
`Login` ➔ `Home Dashboard`  

From the dashboard, users can choose from the following main features:
* **Core Actions:** Scan & Pay, Group Payment, Bills and Bookings, Mobile Recharge
* **Tracking & History:** See Transaction History, Check Bank Balance, Monthly Expense (with interactive analytical charts)
* **Management & Navigation:** Recent Recipients, Search People to Pay, Profile Settings

### 👥 Group Payments Flow
`Create or Select a Group` ➔ `Enter Total Amount` ➔ `Select People` ➔ `Split Expenses` ➔ `Confirm Payment`

### 📷 Scan QR Flow
`Scan QR Code` OR `Upload QR Image From Gallery` ➔ `View Your Own Personal QR Code`

---

## 🎨 Key Design Decisions
* **Trust & Security:** Designed using a modern dark blue-green theme to establish strong visual trust.
* **Streamlined Dashboard:** Quick action shortcuts ensure direct access to the most vital features.
* **Accessible UI Elements:** Employs large touch targets and distinct typography for clear, error-free navigation.
* **Frictionless Payments:** Dedicated search tool combined with a prominent recent recipients row speeds up transfers.
* **Data Visualization:** Integrated visual spending charts summarize monthly transactions at a glance.
